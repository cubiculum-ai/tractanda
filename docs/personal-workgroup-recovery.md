# Personal and workgroup recovery model

The accepted recovery target serves personal, family, and workgroup use. For an explicitly selected personal/workgroup profile, the content baseline is committed item revisions and their owned payloads, interpreted through current OS users, groups, and separately established local runtime configuration. Old databases, indexes, caches, replication bookkeeping, peer secrets, and server-local authority/audit controls are not required recovery inputs for this profile. This rule does not silently convert an existing historical-authority store: ordinary opening requires an established personal profile and returns `personalMigrationRequired` for raw or legacy roots until an explicit selected-content preparation/activation exists.

**Implementation status (source only).** Standalone index/read, saved-document corpus, writable ItemStore core, and Mac product-entrypoint routing have passed separate bounded private suites: 27/27, 14/14, 25/25, and 9/9. Linux product entrypoints separately passed 34/34. The content CLI separately passed four private tests on both Mac and Linux; its conditional filesystem case-alias branch was exercised on Mac but is not evidenced in the Linux log. These results do not combine into one invocation or establish complete product recovery. No installed runtime was changed or activated. New product stores are intended to default to personal/workgroup when profile routing ships. There has been no live activation, migration, or data change.

Each item revision carries its author and revision IDs, explicit parent UUID references, timestamp, owner, group, and current ACL. Changing permissions creates a revision, and the current ACL governs the full history. Tombstones and merge-parent references remain part of that history; timestamps alone do not establish causal ancestry. Unix users and groups define identities.

Fresh-cluster membership remains a later, separate capability. The current personal/workgroup standalone source reports clustering as unsupported and refuses configured peer runtime before opening the service or publishing listeners/readiness. It does not auto-join prior peers. When a future fresh-pairing capability is selected, membership will be administrator-controlled; replicated content cannot change that current configuration. Credentials and endpoints may remain node-local. Pending or otherwise unaccepted staging stays outside the selected committed baseline.

An offline writer's changes are checked against current ACLs and the minimum base references needed to evaluate them. Ambiguity goes to review; the recovery model does not require cumulative authorization snapshots or forensic chains. Small clusters may be reconstructed manually when needed.

Validation for the standalone profile rebuilds derived state from committed content while preserving item identities, history, payloads, and effective ACL behavior. Bounded private suites cover index reconstruction, saved-document content, writable core behavior, Mac/Linux product entrypoints, and Mac/Linux content-CLI methods. Full end-to-end standalone recovery, live activation, and fresh pairing/membership remain pending. A read-only inventory may record source contents; a certified export, separate historical-authority ledger, or extra proof-manifest gate is not required for the personal/workgroup profile. No live reset, migration, deletion, deployment, or replica change is authorized by this document.

Future editions may exchange raw items. Imports preserve common identities, history, content, attachments, and compatible ACL semantics, while establishing an audit baseline at import time without inventing prior audit history. Exports to simpler editions remove enterprise-only audit semantics and disclose lossy mappings. Enterprise implementation and backend choice remain future, undecided work.

The normal source lifecycle, native local-policy method and unsupported-peer startup contract are described in [personal/workgroup entrypoints](personal-workgroup-api.md). These source contracts do not activate or migrate an installed store.
